MILEMATE — FICTIONAL DEMO DATA
==============================

Important notice
----------------
Every name, place, number, schedule, and product detail below is invented for
this project demo. These are not verified real-world claims, services, routes,
or travel advice.

Fictional project overview
-------------------------
- MileMate is a fictional student travel assistance application.
- The example is set at the fictional Northbridge University in the fictional
  city of Alder Bay.
- In this demo, MileMate helps students browse sample travel information and
  save routes for later.
- This demo does not book real travel, process payments, or provide live
  location or transit information.

Fictional demo service details
------------------------------
- The sample dataset contains three fictional bus routes: A1, B2, and C3.
- Route A1 is shown between Northbridge University and Alder Bay Library.
- Route B2 is shown between Northbridge University and Cedar Hall.
- Route C3 is shown between Northbridge University and Harbor Park.
- All three route names and destinations are fictional and exist only within
  this example.
- The fictional demo help desk is available Monday through Friday, 09:00 to
  17:00 Alder Bay demo time.

Fictional demo audience and goals
---------------------------------
- The intended demo audience is students who want to organize example trips.
- A fictional usability exercise uses 20 made-up student profiles.
- The example goal is for at least 16 of those 20 profiles to find and save a
  sample route without assistance.
- These profile counts and goals are illustrative only; no real study or
  measurement has taken place.
